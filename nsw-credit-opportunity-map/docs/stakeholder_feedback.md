# Stakeholder feedback

How the briefing note and maps were tested with readers, and what changed as a result.

## How to collect feedback

1. Send the briefing note PDF and the overview map to 2–4 people. For example:
   - a lecturer or tutor in environmental science or GIS
   - a classmate
   - someone working in planning, ecology or local government
   - a non-specialist (to check that it is easy to read)
2. Ask them three questions:
   - **Is the main message clear within 30 seconds?**
   - **Is anything confusing or missing?**
   - **Would you trust the recommendations? Why or why not?**
3. Record every response below, including ones you decide not to act on, and say why.

## Changes made during development (self-review)

These came from checking the outputs during the build, not from external stakeholders:

| Issue found | Change made |
|---|---|
| Detail map labels overlapped (PCTs 4047 and 3406) | Labelled only the four largest subregions per map, with spread-out offsets |
| Detail maps had no scale | Added a scale bar sized to each map's extent |
| Ranking could look formula-driven | Added a sensitivity test with four weightings; top five unchanged |
